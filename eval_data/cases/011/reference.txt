[Notice on the Renewal and Changes of the KAI-Lounge]

Hello, this is the 36th KAIST Undergraduate Association <SPACE>.

To create a more comfortable campus environment for our fellow students, we have carried out a renewal of the KAI-Lounge in collaboration with the Student Life Team.

Please refer to the details below for more information.

Located between Somang Hall and Sarang Hall, the KAI-Lounge had convenient accessibility and sufficient space but somewhat outdated facilities, leading to continuous requests for improvement.

In response, <SPACE> conducted A Survey for the KAI-Lounge Renewal from May 29 (Thu) to June 5 (Thu).

Based on the survey results, we prepared a proposal outlining the direction of the renovation and submitted it to the Student Life Team.

As a result, the KAI-Lounge renovation has been completed as follows:

<What has changed in the KAI-Lounge?>

- Operating hours extended from 06:00–24:00 to 06:00–02:00 the next day (2-hour extension)

- Additional food waste bins installed

- Wall-mounted tissue dispensers installed

- Recommended volume marked on the digital piano

- Repair and replacement of outdated facilities

<SPACE> will always strive to improve the daily lives of students.

Thank you.

Sincerely,
The 36th KAIST Undergraduate Association <SPACE>
