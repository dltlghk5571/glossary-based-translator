[Background of the Installation]
This past March 2025, the service of "Cube," the existing on-campus unmanned printer vendor, was temporarily suspended due to the company's internal issues.

The contract with "Cube" expired last August, and as it was determined that continuing the service would be difficult, we proceeded with a bidding process for a new vendor.

Recently, after hearing from students that the unmanned printers were being removed and replaced, we contacted the Customer Management Team and requested that they post the locations of the new unmanned multifunction printers on the portal and provide contact information for malfunction/error inquiries.

<SPACE> will always listen to the inconveniences of students and strive to communicate with the university.

Thank you.
