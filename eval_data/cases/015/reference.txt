[Notice of OLEV Timetable Change and Addition of Bus Stops]

Hello, this is the 36th KAIST Undergraduate Association <SPACE>.

Currently, many students use OLEV, the on-campus shuttle bus, to commute to classes or research labs. However, there has been an issue where students find it difficult to attend 1 PM classes held at the Information & Electronics Building or the Applied Engineering Building, as there was no shuttle departing at 12:40 PM.

In addition, the relevant department had been considering adjusting the OLEV service intervals since the beginning of this year due to low ridership in the afternoon, and it was necessary to gather feedback from all students on this matter.

<SPACE> held a meeting with the relevant department last February to inquire about the possibility of adding a 12:40 p.m. departure to the OLEV timetable. The department responded that they had already been considering reducing OLEV operations prior to the discussion and requested that a survey be conducted.

Accordingly, <SPACE> conducted a survey titled 'Survey for Changing the OLEV Operating Schedule' from July 9 (Wed) to July 16 (Wed) to collect preferences regarding the current timetable versus the revised timetable, as well as other opinions about OLEV.

<SPACE> has submitted a report containing the results and analysis of this survey, and as a result, the OLEV timetable was updated and a new stop in the KISTI direction was established on September 1 (Mon).

For detailed information about the revised timetable and the newly added stop, please refer to the images attached to this post.

<SPACE> will always strive to improve the daily lives of students.

Thank you.
