[Shuttle Bus to Daejeon Station & Daejeon Terminal]

Hello, this is <SPACE>, the KAIST Undergraduate Association.

The Daejeon Station / Daejeon Terminal shuttle bus was newly introduced to improve convenience for students traveling to other regions. After two trial operations in September 2024, the shuttle was officially launched in the spring semester of this year.

Originally planned to operate only until the spring semester, the shuttle will continue its service every Friday during this fall semester in response to the strong demand and positive feedback from students.

📅 Operation Period: September 5, 2025 ~ December 19, 2025
🕓 Operation Time: Every Friday (16:00 / 18:30 / 20:00)
🛑 Boarding Location: Central Analysis Center (W8-1)
🛑 Drop-off Locations: In front of Daejeon Station Square, In front of Daejeon Complex Terminal

We kindly ask for your active use of this shuttle service.
