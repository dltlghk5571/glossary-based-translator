[Information on the Improvement of the Dormitory Lounge]

Hello, this is the 36th Undergraduate Association <SPACE>.

The lounges provided on each floor of the dormitories are currently being used for various purposes, including studying, leisure, and rest.

However, in certain lounges, the shortage of desks and chairs has made it difficult for students to fully utilize these spaces. As a result, some students have been inconvenienced by having to seek out lounges on other floors.

In order to create a more pleasant and comfortable lounge environment, <SPACE> conducted A Survey for Improving Dormitory Lounges among students. Based on the survey results, a proposal was drafted and submitted to the Student Life Team.

The following measures have been decided upon after discussion:

<Dormitory Lounge Improvement Measures>
- Inspection and repair of reported facility damages, as requested in the survey
- Replacement of lounge chairs (scheduled for completion in September)

Thank you.

Sincerely,
The 36th Undergraduate Association <SPACE>
