[E11 Course Schedule and Survey on Power Outlet Maintenance in Lecture Rooms]
Hello, this is the 36th KAIST Undergraduate Association <SPACE>.

To improve the aging and malfunctioning power outlets in the lecture rooms of the Creative Learning Building, we are conducting a survey on power outlet maintenance.
During the spring semester, maintenance work for the building’s outlets was requested, and some repairs were carried out over the summer break. However, due to the large number of outlets and the difficulty of checking each one individually, it was found that some outlets were not repaired.

In addition, as multiple reports regarding power outlet issues have been received since the beginning of the fall semester, this survey has been prepared to identify the exact locations and details of the problems.
Survey QR codes are attached inside lecture room doors. If the QR code does not work or any issues occur during the response process, please contact the Undergraduate Association via email (kaistua@student.kaist.ac.kr).

• Survey period: November 3 (Mon) - November 16 (Sun)

The collected responses will be shared with the Facilities Team to ensure that reported power outlet issues are promptly addressed.

Furthermore, to reduce confusion in lecture room usage at the beginning of the semester, class schedules for each lecture room were created and posted in the second week of September.
These schedules will be removed after the semester ends. If you find any errors or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us via email (kaistua@student.kaist.ac.kr).

<SPACE> will continue to work toward providing a more comfortable learning environment for all students.

Thank you.
The 36th KAIST Undergraduate Association <SPACE>
